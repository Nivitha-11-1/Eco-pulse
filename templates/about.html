CIVIC ENVIRONMENTAL PLATFORM

About EcoPulse

Developed by Nivitha R

EcoPulse is an automated civic intelligence platform designed to aggregate, verify, and prioritize environmental, weather, and sustainability news across Tamil Nadu, Chennai, and National India.

🎯

Project Purpose

To provide citizens, campus leaders, urban planners, and environmental advocates with a single, reliable, real-time dashboard of localized ecological alerts, air quality warnings, climate shifts, civic notices, and sustainability initiatives.

⚠️

Problem Statement

Environmental news and civic announcements are highly fragmented across dozens of legacy news portals, university notices, and governmental feeds. Critical air-quality advisories, heatwaves, water contamination alerts, and grassroots campus sustainability drives often get buried beneath general headlines.

PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

Key Features

📡

Continuous Feed Ingestion

Monitors established regional and national environmental information sources with smart deduplication and error isolation.

🤖

AI Relevance Filtering

Evaluates every article using Google Gemini AI or local heuristic reasoning to filter out noise and retain genuine environmental news.

🚦

Priority Classification

Categorizes news into High, Medium, or Low urgency so hazardous events (cyclones, toxic emissions) stand out immediately.

📝

Concise Summarization

Generates actionable 2-3 sentence summaries and recommended citizen actions for rapid comprehension.

⏰

Automated 30-Minute Pipeline

Runs scheduled news aggregation every 30 minutes via in-app APScheduler and automated GitHub Actions.

📱

Responsive Clean UI

Forest-green modern dashboard with interactive search, category filters, and horizontal community carousels.

TRANSPARENCY & BOUNDARIES

Current System Limitations

  • Event Booking & Registration: EcoPulse aggregates news reports of community campaigns and events; it does not host interactive ticket sales or live attendee registration forms. Users are linked to original publishers for registration details.
  • Public Runner Storage: GitHub Actions runners run in ephemeral virtual machines. Updates performed solely inside a workflow runner require synchronization with shared persistent storage to update the live public site.
  • AI Rate Limits: The free-tier Gemini API can hit RPM/TPD limits during bulk runs. The pipeline seamlessly utilizes local heuristic rule fallbacks so no feed collection is dropped.
  • Feed Metadata Variations: Some legacy news outlets omit standardized geographical tags or pubdates in their RSS feeds, falling back to article collection timestamps.
